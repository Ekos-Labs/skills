# Market sizing rules

- Prefer, in order: official statistics (Eurostat, national statistics offices, US Census), regulators and filings, then established research houses (Gartner, IDC, Forrester, Statista with its cited source, Grand View, Mordor, Fortune Business Insights). Avoid press releases that resell paywalled numbers without a method.
- Quote one global figure. State publisher, year of the estimate, the year it refers to, and the link.
- SAM = TAM x the share that matches the product's geography, segment and model. SOM = SAM x a realistic three-year share for a company of this size (usually 0.5 to 3 percent for a new entrant). Write each percentage and the reason.
- Currency: keep the source currency and add a converted figure with the rate and date.
- If two sources disagree by more than 2x, show both and explain which you used.

## Channel fit

Keep a channel only if most of these hold:

1. The buyer already gathers there (evidence link).
2. The founder can reach them without a large budget in the first month.
3. The message can show proof in that format (demo, numbers, story).
4. Feedback arrives within two weeks (replies, signups, calls).
5. It does not depend on a single platform rule the founder cannot control.
