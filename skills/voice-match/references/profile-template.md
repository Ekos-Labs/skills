# Voice profile template

```markdown
# Voice profile: <name>
Samples used: <titles and dates>
## Rhythm: sentence length, paragraph length, line breaks
## Openings: how pieces start (quote examples)
## Endings: how pieces end
## Words they use / words they never use
## Proof style: numbers, stories, screenshots
## Tone: direct / warm / funny / dry, with examples
## Formatting habits
## Do not: things that would sound wrong from this person
```

## AI tells

Remove: em dashes used as connectors; "Here's the thing", "Let's dive in", "In today's fast-paced world", "It's not just X, it's Y"; "delve", "tapestry", "landscape", "leverage", "game-changer", "unlock"; lists of exactly three adjectives; every paragraph the same length; a question at the end added for engagement; summary sentences that repeat the paragraph; excessive hedging ("it's important to note").
