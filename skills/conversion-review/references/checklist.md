# Page review checklist

- Does the first screen say what this is, for whom, and what to do next?
- Is there one primary action, and is it visible without scrolling on mobile?
- Does the page load fast (under about 2.5 s for the main content on mobile)?
- Do forms ask only for what is needed? Do errors explain what to fix?
- Is proof near the action (numbers, logos, reviews), and is it real?
- Do pricing and the action match what the ad or link promised?
- Are there distractions on the conversion page (nav links, pop-ups, chat widgets)?
- Does the page work on a small phone (390 px) without overflow?
