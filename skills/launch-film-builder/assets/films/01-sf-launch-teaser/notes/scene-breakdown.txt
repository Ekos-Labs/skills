3. Notes Carried Over From the Whop Model
	•	No voiceover anywhere in this structure — every beat is either a real artefact (photo, broadcast, screen recording) or on-screen typography. If narration gets added later, it should replace at most one line, not become the spine.
	•	Black is used exactly twice — Scene 7 ("Bull Pen Era — 1999") and Scene 16 onward (the doubt sequence). Reusing black more often than that flattens its impact; the whole reason it works in Act V is that the audience has only seen it once before.
	•	Every historical or news beat (Acts I–IV) should use the real artefact — actual archival photos, actual broadcast footage, actual tool interfaces — not recreations. This is the single biggest driver of why the Whop film reads as authored rather than stock.
	•	Keep one continuous score/music bed running under Acts I–IV; let Act V's silence-adjacent stillness (black screen, no music swell) be the contrast that makes the reversal land.
	•	The founders' circle in Scene 19 is the one moment built specifically for this film rather than borrowed from the Whop structure — treat it as the visual signature, since it's the beat with no direct precedent in the reference film.
