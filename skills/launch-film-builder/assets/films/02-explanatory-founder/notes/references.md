# Reference Board

## Primary motion reference

Daniel Chhetri launch film:
https://x.com/chddaniel/status/2095775049475313943?s=46

Use the reference for its motion grammar:

- A new visual idea every two to five seconds.
- Giant type for one important phrase, rather than animated paragraphs.
- Translucent cards arranged in depth.
- Continuous camera pushes that connect adjacent scenes.
- Product interfaces used as proof, not decoration.
- Cursor movement only when it explains an action.
- A restrained brand close after a dense middle section.

Do not reproduce its footage, layouts, music, or individual graphics. The Ekos
film must use the real website, engine architecture, process, products, proof,
and brand system.

## Visual principle

The talking head supplies trust. Remotion reveals the system operating around
Eshaan. Graphics should respond to what he is saying: context enters, the
engine configures itself, channels activate, proof accumulates, and the
products emerge from the same system.
