# Footage Handoff

## Send when the edit is ready

1. The clean 16:9 talking-head edit at its original resolution and frame rate.
2. The same edit without music, sound effects, captions, transitions, zooms, or
   colour filters. Straight cuts are fine.
3. The isolated dialogue WAV if your editor can export it.
4. Ten seconds of room tone.
5. The opening AI news clips if you have selected them, including their source
   or licence information.
6. Any moments where you definitely want the camera to remain visible.

ProRes 422 is preferred for the clean video. A high-bitrate H.264 or H.265 file
is also workable. Do not send the file through a messaging app that recompresses
it.

## Helpful but optional

- The full uncut take, in case a transition needs extra handles.
- A project XML or EDL from the talking-head edit.
- The exact font used for any editorial captions you want retained.
- A music track you already like, shared with its licence details.

## What can wait

No final music selection or sound-effect purchases are needed before the visual
timing is built. The current audio brief is enough to design the animation and
select candidates after the dialogue timing is locked.
