# Storyboard — Explanatory Launch Film

Target: approximately 90 seconds, with exact timing taken from the edited
performance. Build the master at 3840×2160 in 16:9, using one continuous
talking-head performance as the spine. The Daniel reference supplies the
composition grammar, not the aspect ratio.

## Edit rhythm

- First two seconds: 4–6 frame flashes and hard sonic cuts.
- Talking-head sections: fewer cuts, with movement coming from restrained crops.
- Remotion sections: every sentence causes one visible state change.
- Use one continuous virtual camera through the process and engine sequence so it
  feels like one product, not a slideshow of website sections.
- Hold the final card for at least 2.5 seconds.
