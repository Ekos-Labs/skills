Performance structure: one continuous 16:9 talking-head recording. The news
montage, Remotion graphics, product sequences, proof cards, and transitions are
layered over or cut into that performance.

The vertical bars mark natural breathing points. They are not edit points.

## Recording instructions

- Record the complete script as one 16:9 talking-head performance in 4K.
- Keep the camera locked and frame slightly wider than the final crop so the
  edit has room for product cards and motion graphics beside you.
- Record each section separately after the complete take. These pickups give
  the editor clean options without changing the visual setup.
- Use your live talking-head audio throughout. Remotion can temporarily cover
  the frame while your speech continues underneath.
- Speak naturally through each thought. The vertical bars are breathing points,
  not full dramatic pauses.
- Leave two seconds of silence before and after every take.
- Record ten seconds of room tone after the final take.
- Record one safety take of each section with slightly less intensity.
