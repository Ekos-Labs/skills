# Eshaan's Original Explanatory Launch Script

Recovered from Eshaan's own messages in the original launch-video session.
Spelling is cleaned for readability; the ideas, sequence, speaker handoffs, and
visual intent are preserved.

## Opening — news montage

Three or four rapid news clips, each saying:

> AI. AI. AI. AI.

## Eshaan — on camera, mixed with AI-building screens

> AI has made building so easy. We type in a prompt and get something out in no
> time, and that is how companies start these days.
>
> But when it comes to selling that idea, it fails, because the biggest
> companies are not built by prompts. They are built by distribution,
> visibility, and trust.
>
> And that cannot be prompted. It is designed.
>
> And that is why I am building Ekos Labs.

Visual intent: mix Eshaan's face with real screens of Lovable one-shotting a
build, a chat bar being typed into, and Claude/Codex responding. The same visual
pattern continues until “That is why I am building Ekos Labs.”

## AI voiceover — Remotion product explanation

> At Ekos Labs, we are building customized GTM engines and products for
> companies and founders, because every company deserves a playbook built just
> for them.
>
> We diagnose the work, understand the ICPs, and create the segments. Then we
> plan the whole build, decide which channels to use and what to implement, and
> run the engine.
>
> We double down on what the data shows, and you are left with a GTM engine
> built for you within weeks.
>
> And not just that. We are launching products so any solo founder can scale
> their distribution without relying on anyone else.

Visual intent: this entire section is motion-led. Go deeper into understanding,
building, and running the engine. Use real website material, content-machine
visuals, product cards, Screen Studio captures, and native Remotion animation.

## Eshaan — back on camera

> I have a track record of more than twenty million organic views, thousands of
> app downloads for clients, and conversions happening in the backend through
> systems designed specifically for them.
>
> That is what I bring to Ekos Labs.

Eshaan specified a final AI voiceover and logo close, but the final words were
never written or approved.
