# Message rules

- Lead with them, not with the product. The first sentence is about their situation.
- One fact, stated plainly. Not "I noticed you are crushing it".
- No fake familiarity ("Hope you're well", "Quick question" subject lines, "Just following up").
- No buzzwords: leverage, synergy, streamline, revolutionise, game-changer, cutting-edge.
- No em dashes. Short sentences.
- One question that is easy to answer with one line.
- Follow-ups add something new; never "bumping this to the top of your inbox".
- If the reader would be embarrassed to see how the fact was found, do not use it.
