# Spoken format and voice

The creator records straight off the script. The script is the thing they read out loud. If they cannot read it top to bottom and sound like themselves, it failed.

## Format (non-negotiable)

- **Line by line.** One spoken sentence per line, each one natural breath.
- **No labels inside the script.** Never "HOOK:", "STORY:", "TURN:", "CTA:" or "point 2". They record words, not your outline.
- **No stage directions inside the lines.** No "(show screenshot)" or "(cut to B-roll)". All footage, overlay and edit notes go in a separate block below the script.
- **No paragraphs.** A clean vertical list of the exact lines and nothing else.
- **No em dashes, ever.** They read as written copy. Use commas and "so" or "but".
- The clean script comes first in the reply, so it can be pasted straight into a teleprompter.

## Length

40 to 50 seconds by default. Many creators speak at about 4 words a second, so that is 160 to 200 words. Measure the creator's own pace from one of their videos (words divided by seconds) and use it, rather than a generic rate of 2.5 to 3. Cut beats to fit; do not run long.

## Voice: learn it from their own words

Before writing lines, read two or three of the creator's own transcripts or voice notes. Not their polished posts: written voice and spoken voice differ. Notice and keep:
- **How they chain thoughts.** Many people talk in one breath with "and", "so" and "but" instead of tidy separate sentences. Keep that.
- **Repeated words.** If they say "real" or "build" again and again, keep the repetition. Swapping in synonyms makes it sound written.
- **Their tics** ("literally", "honestly", "basically"), kept at their real frequency.
- **Flat self-assessment.** "I knew I wasn't going back", not "I started to realise maybe...".
- **Verbs in sequence, not lists of roles.** "I was broke, started a business, failed, moved countries", not "a broke kid, a founder, a failure".

When the creator gives their own words, keep them. Light cleanup only (typos, flow). Never rewrite their line into your copy.

## Test every line

- Could they say it cold, in one take, without reciting? If it needs exact recall, loosen it.
- Any "a X, a Y, a Z" listing, em-dash reveal, synonym swap, or a slip into the third person ("building his company" instead of "my company")? Rewrite it.
- When you offer alternative lines, write them the way the creator would say them, not as polished marketing options.
