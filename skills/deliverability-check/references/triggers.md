# Spam triggers to flag

- More than one link in a first cold email; link shorteners; open or click tracking on a new domain.
- Attachments in a first email.
- Subject in all caps, excessive punctuation (!!!, ???), or "Re:" / "Fwd:" on a first message.
- Phrases: free, guarantee, act now, limited time, no obligation, risk-free, earn money, 100%.
- Image-only emails or large images, HTML with heavy styling.
- Mismatch between the from name and the signature.
- No way to opt out where required.
