# Positioning checklist

1. **Category.** Can a stranger name what kind of product this is?
2. **Buyer.** Is it clear who it is for, specifically enough that some visitors know it is not for them?
3. **Outcome.** Does the headline state a result the buyer wants, not a feature?
4. **Alternative.** Is it clear what the buyer does today instead, and why this is better?
5. **Proof.** Is the strongest real proof (numbers, names, demos) above the fold or one scroll away?
6. **Specifics.** Are there numbers, examples or screenshots instead of adjectives?
7. **One action.** Is there one primary call to action that matches the buyer's stage?
8. **No inside language.** Is every term one the buyer would use, not an internal name?
