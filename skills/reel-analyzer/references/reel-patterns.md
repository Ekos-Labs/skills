# Reel pattern names

Use these names in the breakdown's Hook, Bridge, Conflict, Story shape and Ending fields. They were read from the structure of short-form talking-head and explainer reels that beat their account's follower count. Skits, interview clips and pure scenes follow different rules: say so instead of forcing a name. If nothing fits, write "none of the listed patterns" and describe it in one line.

## Hook (0 to 3 seconds)

The first line carries a contradiction or a gap.

| Name | Shape |
|---|---|
| Result plus unexpected state | A big result next to a state that should not produce it ("[result] while I was [ordinary state]"). |
| Shock statement plus identity stack | A bold decision, then who the speaker is in two or three facts. |
| Age plus status | Age and a status that does not fit it, in the first line. Makes the viewer a peer. |
| Question people ask | "Everyone asks me [X]" or a scenario question the viewer has. |
| Contrarian opener | "Is it just me or [X] is overrated", "you want the truth", "it's not what you think". |
| Audience plus guide plus enemy list | "[Audience] edition. No [A], no [B], just the real version." |
| Offer first | A specific offer with every barrier removed, then proof, steps and CTA. |
| Second-person relatable list | "You do [A], you feel [B]..." ending on "if that's you, I'm [name]". |
| Reaction or quote | "Someone said [X] and I sat there for a second." |
| One-liner reversal | A quoted belief and a flip, in a very short reel. |
| News hook | "[Company] just revealed [X]." |
| Emotional repetition | The same sentence start three or four times. No tactics. |

## Bridge (about 3 to 10 seconds, 1 to 2 sentences)

Turns the hook's tension into a promise and names what is coming. Reels under about 40 seconds often skip it.

| Name | Shape |
|---|---|
| Time or cost anchor | "[N] months ago..." or "it cost me [X]". |
| Reframe promise | "This changes how you think about X. It's not A, it's B." |
| Belief reversal | "Most people do X. That's the wrong approach." |
| Stakes | "This matters, because if X, then Y." |
| Answer tease | "The answer is simple." |
| Personal position | "I don't think I have to pick one." |
| Polarity | "Listen or don't, I don't care." |

## Conflict (one named obstacle, never several)

False belief to overturn (most common). Outsider who cannot get in, solved by a method. Either/or pressure. The viewer's own pain. Origin gap. Broken system. Cost or sacrifice. Hollow win. Valley (kicked out, embarrassed, rejected). Success looks effortless.

## Story shapes

| Name | Beats |
|---|---|
| Proof mechanism | Claim, obstacle, method in steps, proof on screen, so-what. Demos and tutorials. |
| Reframe | Question, reversal, analogy or evidence list, one-line instruction. Often 25 to 60 seconds. |
| Ranked or numbered list | Items easiest to hardest, each with requirement, benefit and catch, then "save this". |
| Lifestyle proof | Proof, then cause, then origin, then an action for the viewer. |
| Pure emotion | Repeated line, purpose, no tactics. |
| News or discovery | What it is, a one-line analogy, why it matters, tie to the viewer. |
| Age-stamped timeline | Milestones by age with one valley and one turn. The ages are the open loops. |
| Icon then equaliser | "This person also started from zero", then "now go work at it". |

## Retention devices

- Open loops: numbered steps, "next up", a withheld secret or a one-word teaser. A new loop about every 10 seconds.
- Proof shown on screen around the midpoint (messages, dashboards, replies), not only told.
- Specific numbers in most lines.
- Payoff by about two-thirds of the runtime, then a short closing line.
- A visual change every few seconds: cutaway, icon, caption word, zoom.

## Endings

Keyword CTA ("comment X"), free-guide CTA, "save this", "follow, the story continues", an instruction to the viewer, a punchline, or a closing meaning with no CTA.

## Length

Personal story reels cluster around 55 to 70 seconds, with a median near 60. Demos, tours and day logs run 90 to 140 seconds.

## Pattern check (use when judging what transfers)

1. First line has a contradiction or gap.
2. Over about 45 seconds: a 1 to 2 sentence bridge (promise, reversal, stakes or cost).
3. One named conflict.
4. Resolution by proof, numbers or a ranked list, with a new loop about every 10 seconds.
5. A clear close: keyword, instruction, follow, or a closing meaning.
6. One polarity target, picked on purpose (the default path, tool obsession, common advice, low-effort AI content, a broken system).
