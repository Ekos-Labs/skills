# Fixes that tend to change AI answers

1. A clear "What is <brand>" paragraph on the homepage and about page: category, buyer, key facts, in plain text (not only in images).
2. A pricing page with current, crawlable prices.
3. Comparison and alternatives pages that are honest and specific.
4. Consistent name, description and facts across the site, docs, LinkedIn, Crunchbase, GitHub and directories.
5. Being listed on the third-party pages the assistants already cite (category roundups, review sites), by earning the mention, not buying spam.
6. FAQ content that answers the exact buyer questions in one or two sentences each.
7. Structured data (Organization, Product, FAQ) and an llms.txt file summarising the site.
8. Making sure important pages are not blocked to crawlers that power these assistants (check robots.txt).
